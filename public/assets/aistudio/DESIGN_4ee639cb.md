## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a gentle, reassuring, and deeply empathetic sanctuary for menstrual health, fertility, and hormonal wellness tracking. It departs from sterile, clinical medical aesthetics and generic candy-pink cliches, instead embracing an elevated, tactile, and intimate environment where daily tracking feels like a grounding self-care ritual.

### Emotional Target & Audience
- **Target Audience:** Modern individuals tracking menstrual, ovulation, hormonal, and emotional patterns who seek biological clarity, proactive health awareness, and visual comfort.
- **Tone & Mood:** Warm, trustworthy, intimate, calm, and exquisitely crafted. The interface evokes peace, physical comfort, and soft reassurance while maintaining rigorous scientific legibility.
- **Aesthetic Direction:** Soft Claymorphic & Tactile Minimalism. The visual identity leverages velvety blush surfaces, organic curves, pill silhouettes, airy hairline borders, and warm tinted glows. Interactive elements feel like smooth tumbled river stones, soft clay tokens, and delicate frosted glassware.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in comforting rose tones, deep raspberry accents, and grounding plum-wine neutrals. Surfaces are warm and light, preventing eye strain while fostering high emotional warmth.

### Color Tiers & Application
- **Primary (`#D81B60` - Deep Raspberry):** The signature interactive focus. Reserved for high-priority calls to action, active cycle status indicators, high-fertility highlights, and primary state markers. Interactive press states shift to `#C2185B` and `#AD1457`.
- **Secondary (`#F8BBD0` - Petal Pink):** Soft, soothing tint used for tonal fills, secondary interactive states, calendar period indicator fills, subtle badges, and inactive toggle tracks.
- **Tertiary (`#4A0E2E` - Rich Plum Wine):** Grounding contrast accent. Used for high-contrast informational cards, phase milestone badges, evening cycle insights, and deep emphasis markers.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FFF4F6` base with `#FDEEF2` secondary wash):** The overarching atmosphere. Tinted blush white replaces cold grey canvas backgrounds.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container surfaces layered on top of blush tints, producing a luminous, clean, and crisp presentation.
- **Typography & Details:**
  - *Primary Headings & Key Data:* `#2D1622` (Deep Warm Charcoal-Plum) providing accessible, high-contrast readability with soft warmth.
  - *Secondary & Body Subtext:* `#7A5C6D` (Rosy Slate) for non-jarring supplementary information.
  - *Subtle Borders:* `#FCE4EC` with alpha values between 40% and 80%.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans provides a friendly, welcoming, geometric curve profile that complements the ultra-rounded, soft clay interface.

### Hierarchy & Typesetting Guidelines
- **Numerical Focal Points (`display`):** For cycle day counters (e.g., "Day 14") and countdown numbers, use heavy weights (`800`) paired with tight negative letter tracking to create clear visual anchors.
- **Headings (`headline-lg`, `headline-md`):** Use for phase titles, cycle predictions, and section headers. Keep tracking slightly negative (`-0.02em`) to maintain cohesion.
- **Body Text:** Standard body scales prioritize legible line heights (`1.5`) in `#2D1622` for standard text and `#7A5C6D` for helper descriptions, preventing harsh black-on-white contrast.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase or small tracking adjustments must remain subtle. Avoid clinical condensed styling; maintain natural, open letter forms.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around breathing room, soft card clusters, and focused mobile-first single-column ergonomics that expand gracefully to tablets.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column stack with dynamic fluid padding. Fluid full-width cards with `1.25rem` screen edge margins ensure quick thumb access without horizontal edge friction.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid with `1.5rem` gutters. Cycle rings and timeline visualizers hold a persistent central or side hero position, while symptom tracking tiles form a flexible 2-column cluster.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column structured grid centered within a max-width container (`1140px`) to preserve intimacy and prevent sparse or stretched layouts.

### Spacing Principles
- Use vertical breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) between distinct analytical modules (e.g., Cycle Phase Ring, Symptom Logger, Daily Insights).
- Internal component groupings (such as chip clusters or horizontal date pickers) use tighter tokens (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to form intuitive tactile clusters.
- Never place elements directly against screen edges; all modules sit inset within the designated canvas margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on soft claymorphic dimensionality, tinted raspberry ambient diffusion, and subtle inset highlights rather than flat grey drop shadows.

### Dimensionality System
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Delicate tinted background gradient transitioning smoothly from `#FFF4F6` at top to `#FDEEF2` at base.
- **Layer 1 (Luminous Floating Cards):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) cards with a double-layered blush shadow:
  - Ambient: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(216, 27, 96, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(216, 27, 96, 0.05)`
  - Border: Hairline stroke `1px solid rgba(248, 187, 208, 0.45)`
- **Layer 2 (Elevated Tactile Tokens & Buttons):** Raised interactive pills with tactile depth:
  - Primary Button Shadow: `0 8px 20px -3px rgba(216, 27, 96, 0.32), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(216, 27, 96, 0.18)`
  - Specular Inset: `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` creating a soft clay/curved pebble reflection along the top perimeter.
- **Layer 3 (Floating Nav Bar & Drawers):** Glassmorphic pill nav bar:
  - Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`
  - Backdrop Blur: `20px blur`
  - Shadow: `0 14px 34px -6px rgba(74, 14, 46, 0.12)`
  - Active Tab Button: Raised circular bubble featuring `#D81B60` background, white icon, and a deep raspberry bloom shadow (`0 6px 16px rgba(216, 27, 96, 0.45)`).
- **Pressed State:** Shadow blurs decrease and internal inset shadows (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`) activate, providing direct physical feedback.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses the `3` (Pill-shaped) roundedness profile to generate organic, biological, and comforting forms across every touchpoint.

### Geometric Expression
- **Pill Philosophy:** Every button, chip, text field, calendar cell selection, and navigation tab uses full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / `border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Cards & Visual Modules:** Containers apply generous `rounded-3xl` radii (`2rem` / `32px`), giving surfaces the appearance of smooth, tactile stones.
- **Circular Wave Ring Motifs:** Visualizations (cycle progress trackers, fertility gauges, and hydration trackers) use concentric circles, organic curved wave sweeps, and soft teardrop indicators to mirror natural physiological rhythms.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action Button:** Fully rounded pill (`rounded-full`), `#D81B60` background, pure white bold text, internal top highlight (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.7)`), and raspberry shadow (`0 10px 22px -4px rgba(216,27,96,0.3)`).
- **Secondary / Soft Button:** Pill form factor with soft blush tint (`#FDEEF2`), text in `#D81B60`, and hairline border (`1px solid #F8BBD0`).
- **Tactile Circle Icon Button:** Symmetrical 48x48px or 56x56px circular action buttons for logging symptoms, quick notes, or navigation triggers.

### Chips & Symptom Tags
- **Default State:** Pill shape, white background, `1px solid rgba(248, 187, 208, 0.5)`, subtext color `#7A5C6D`. Contains micro-emoji or line icon alongside descriptor (e.g., "Mild Cramps", "Energetic", "Tender").
- **Selected State:** Shifts to `#D81B60` or `#FDEEF2` fill with `#D81B60` border and text. Elevated with a slight blush drop shadow (`0 4px 12px rgba(216,27,96,0.18)`).

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Rounded pill container with `#FFFFFF` background, subtle blush inner glow, and a `1px solid #F8BBD0` default border.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#D81B60` with a soft outer ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(216, 27, 96, 0.12)`).
- **Text & Placeholder:** Input text is typed in `#2D1622`; placeholder text rests in `#A88B9C`.

### Cards & Surfaces
- **Standard Insight Card:** `#FFFFFF` background, `rounded-3xl` corners, delicate `1px` border (`#FCE4EC`), and soft ambient raspberry shadow.
- **Hero Cycle Phase Card:** Can alternate between pure white with concentric animated ring graphics or deep plum `#4A0E2E` with luminous white and blush text for cycle transitions.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular bubble (`rounded-full`) with a soft rose outline (`2px solid #F8BBD0`). When checked, renders a smooth animated inner pill in `#D81B60` with an inset specular gleam.
- **Checkboxes:** Squircle/pill hybrid (`rounded-xl`), toggling to `#D81B60` fill with a crisp white checkmark icon.

### Floating Bottom Navigation Bar
- A floating pill navigation capsule centered at the bottom of the screen with a `1.25rem` bottom and lateral margin.
- Translucent frosted glass effect (`backdrop-blur-md` over `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), hairline border (`rgba(248, 187, 208, 0.5)`).
- Inactive items display soft plum-slate icons (`#7A5C6D`). The active item transitions into an elevated circular pill button (`#D81B60`) with a blooming raspberry glow, elevating the active view icon cleanly above the bar.

### Specialized Health Tracker Modules
- **Concentric Cycle Ring Tracker:** Central circular hero displaying cycle days left, current biological phase (e.g., "Follicular Phase"), and predictive ovulation windows via glowing gradient arcs.
- **Horizontal Date Scroller:** Rounded floating ribbon showing the active week; current day is highlighted with a vertical pill card in `#D81B60`.
